Your profile on Volanta has to be public. Nothing is stored anywhere except your own browser.

Volanta only shares data with its own website, so your flights are collected from a Volanta tab and handed back here. It takes three clicks:

  1. 1
    One-time setup: drag this button to your bookmarks bar.
    ✈ Send to Portfolio No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + B.
  2. 2
  3. 3
    On the Volanta tab, click ✈ Send to Portfolio in your bookmarks bar. Your flights appear here automatically.
